# NEON RIFT: STARLIGHT RESCUE — Submission packet

## Game description

Explore twelve glowing star sectors in a peaceful space adventure. Pilot a nimble ship through drifting asteroid fields, collect starlight, and restore three ancient beacons in every sector to open the next rift. Unlock gentle ship upgrades that improve gliding, protection, and starlight collection.

## Short description

A neon space exploration adventure. Collect starlight, restore ancient beacons, and discover twelve glowing sectors.

## Controls

- Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to steer.
- Shift: short boost.
- Space or P: pause or resume.
- Mouse: click and drag the playfield to steer; click Boost to accelerate.
- Touch: drag the playfield to steer; tap Boost to accelerate.
- Use the on-screen Pause button at any time during play.

## Gameplay loop

1. Collect glowing starlight orbs.
2. Bring at least three energy units to an inactive beacon.
3. Stay close until the beacon is fully restored.
4. Restore all three beacons to complete the sector.
5. Choose an optional ship upgrade and continue through the twelve sectors.

## Content and feature notes

- Single-player, browser-native HTML5 Canvas / JavaScript game.
- Twelve sectors with increasing asteroid density.
- Non-combat progression: no weapons, shooting, enemies, boss fights, or hostile targeting.
- Keyboard, mouse, and touch input.
- Progress saving with a visible notice when persistent browser storage is unavailable.
- CrazyGames SDK v3 integration and Poki SDK v2 integration are isolated in separate builds.
