TITLE
NEON RIFT

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Fly close to danger. Build your own starfighter across twelve sectors, chain lightning through swarms and dash through three powerful guardians.

DESCRIPTION
NEON RIFT is a neon space survival shooter with automatic weapons and responsive movement. Chain fast eliminations for score multipliers, read the enemy tells and dash through danger with temporary protection.

Unlock twelve sectors in order. Each sector ends with three guardians: Sentinel, Hive Mother and Voidkeeper. The later campaign mixes sniper crossfire, charging hunters, splitting swarms and minefield sieges.

During each flight, choose from three upgrades. Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary cards change the power of your weapons. Combine rapid fire, chain lightning, orbital drones or a damaging dash to find your own build.

Between flights, spend earned rift cores on eighteen permanent skills. Choose specialist systems such as Storm Heart, Last Spark or Star Collector. Reset your skill tree for a full refund whenever you want to try a new build.

Progress saves automatically. An active flight can be resumed, and save files can be exported and imported. Sound effects, particles and camera shake are adjustable.

CONTROLS
WASD or arrow keys: move.
Space: dash with brief invulnerability.
P or Escape: pause and resume.
1, 2 or 3: choose an upgrade.
Touch: drag anywhere in the arena like a joystick; tap the lightning button to dash.
Weapons fire automatically.

SUGGESTED TAGS

Responsive portrait and landscape layouts. Local fonts and generated audio.

Static covers are promotional artwork, not gameplay screenshots.
Browser/device playtesting and real SDK QA remain required before release.
